Cartography

Jack Schroeder

A collection of maps built with ArcGIS and Adobe Illustrator. All maps were created in an Introduction to Cartography class at UW-Madison. These maps cover different scales, from the state level to a country and continent level. They explore a variety of different personal and academic interests, such as environmental issues, tourism and political features.
